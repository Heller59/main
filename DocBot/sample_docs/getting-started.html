Getting Started with Acme Widgets

Acme Widgets is a platform for managing customer orders. This guide covers account setup, creating your first order, and common troubleshooting steps.

Creating an Account

To create an account, click the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner of the site. You will need a valid email address and company name. After signing up, check your email for a verification link. Accounts must be verified within 24 hours or the signup will expire.

Creating Your First Order

Once logged in, navigate to the Orders tab and click "New Order". Select a customer from the dropdown, add one or more line items, and click Submit. Orders in "Draft" status can be edited freely. Once submitted, an order moves to "Pending Review" and can only be edited by users with the Approver role.

Order Statuses

Orders move through the following statuses: Draft, Pending Review, Approved, Shipped, and Completed. Only Admin users can move an order directly from Draft to Approved, skipping review.

Troubleshooting

If an order is stuck in Pending Review for more than 48 hours, contact your account's designated Approver. If you do not know who your Approver is, check the Team Settings page under Roles and Permissions.
