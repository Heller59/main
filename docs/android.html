Get the app on Android

The app is free and in early testing on Google Play. Three quick steps get you in. Use the same Google account for all three, the one your phone’s Play Store uses. Each button opens in a new tab, so this page stays open.

  1. Step 1

    Join the app’s Google Group. Tap Join group near the top of the page.

    Open the Google Group
  2. Step 2

    Switch back to this tab, then tap Become a tester on the Google Play page.

    Become a tester
  3. Step 3

    Install the app from Google Play.

    Install from Google Play

If something doesn’t work

  • “App not available” in step 2 or 3. Wait a few minutes after joining the group, then try again. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Google account you used to join.
  • More than one Google account? In the Play Store, tap your profile picture and switch to the account that joined the group.

On an iPhone?

Install Apple’s TestFlight app, then open the TestFlight invite.
