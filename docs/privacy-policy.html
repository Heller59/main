Privacy Policy

The Orchards Trash & Recycling · Last updated October 30, 2026

The Orchards Trash & Recycling ("the app") is an independent, resident-built app that shows trash, recycling, and bulk pickup schedules for the Orchards at Greentree community in Evesham Township, NJ, and sends optional local reminders. It is not operated or endorsed by the Orchards at Greentree HOA.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network requests that send data about you anywhere.

Data stored on your device

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device only, using standard device storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Whether reminder notifications are turned on or off
  • How many days before a pickup you'd like to be reminded
  • Your selected pickup zone

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications directly on your device using your device's operating system. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device — no notification content or scheduling data is sent to any server.

Third parties

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a revised "Last updated" date.
