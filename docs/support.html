The Orchards Trash & Recycling

The Orchards Trash & Recycling is an independent, resident-built app that shows trash, recycling, and bulk pickup schedules for the Orchards at Greentree community in Marlton, NJ, and sends optional local reminders.

Common questions

  • My pickup dates look wrong. Check your zone under Settings — pickup day can vary by street.
  • I'm not getting reminder notifications. Make sure reminders are turned on in Settings, and that notifications are allowed for the app in your device's system settings.
  • Does this app work without the HOA? Yes — the schedule is built into the app and does not require signing in or an internet connection.
